- Deep experience building and shipping production AI Agents.
- Strong system design and architecture skills across infrastructure, data modeling, APIs, and monorepos.
- Able to 10x your workflow with AI coding tools while maintaining high code quality.
- Product sense: experience shipping to customers and iterating based on feedback.
- Strong experience with TypeScript, React, Git, containers, Postgres, and Redis.
- Comfortable talking with users, proposing ideas, and shipping autonomously.
